App name
ROUSTAN ROBERT

Short description
Run local quality checks, resources, leads, and shipment logs from one operations board.

Full description
ROUSTAN ROBERT helps small business teams keep daily operations organized on one Android device. The app brings quality control checklists, resource allocation notes, lead follow-up, and shipment status into a focused mobile workflow.

Teams can review the daily command board, open checklist details, add new quality checks, track lead stages, and monitor dispatch risks without account registration or cloud sync.
